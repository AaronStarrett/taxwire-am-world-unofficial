Taxwire AM training export, schema version 1

The two JSON examples are synthetic fixtures. They are not actual learner saves
or exports produced by the independent Academy edition. Passing these fixtures
establishes schema compatibility only. Real cross-app interoperability remains
unverified until a genuine Academy export is tested.

Training files carry portable history, work products, review plans, edition
provenance, and case fingerprints. They do not resume an engine-specific 3D scene.
Imports are self-reported history, not an official credential. Matching a mission
family ID alone cannot pass a different local case. Unknown extension fields are
retained. Imports are bounded to 2 MiB and do not replace newer local progress.

World save export/import is a separate versioned format with its own validation.
Never commit actual learner files, personal notes, or private work to this public
repository. Browser saves are local to the browser profile and origin. Localhost,
GitHub Pages, other browser profiles, and other devices do not automatically
share saves. Use export/import to move portable progress deliberately.
