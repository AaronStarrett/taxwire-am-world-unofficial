# Known limits

- Qualified tax/legal and employer procedure review: **NOT RUN**. All real tax reference summaries retain professional-review-pending status. This is educational practice, not tax advice, production onboarding approval, or certification.
- Actual Academy interoperability: **NOT RUN**. Synthetic fixtures pass the shared contract; no real other-edition file was available. Imports preserve self-reported provenance and case fingerprints.
- No actual tax filing/payment/customer system/provider acceptance is tested or implied. Later verification documents are authored fictional outcomes.
- Renderer measurements describe the tested browser/hardware/renderer only. Integrated-GPU interactive play and CI software rendering are different evidence. Universal i5/30-FPS guarantees, mobile 3D optimization and full assistive-technology audit are not claimed.
- NPCs provide authored finite knowledge, dialogue decisions and commitment memory. They do not simulate autonomous offscreen tax work or predictive psychology. Characters are original procedural semi-realistic models, rather than photorealistic scans; nonessential voice is not implemented. No microphone is requested.
- The inbox includes authored case messages and locally recorded updates; business changes are represented by seeded events and case arcs. It is a compact teaching simulation, not an enterprise CRM or continuously evolving economy.
- Local authoring validates and exports a candidate but does not activate unreviewed content into grading. No secure instructor access, cloud saves, multi-user accounts or employer scoreboard exists.
- Browser-local profiles and export/import work; saves are separate across localhost, hosted origin and devices. Browser/OS/Codex-managed writes outside D: cannot be fully controlled by this application.
- A reopened/copied synthetic Chrome test profile crashed the browser process during native download. Fresh-profile native backup, import and full twelve-case export/readback passed; the copied-profile crash's browser-internal cause remains unidentified. It is not counted as successful native-export evidence.
- Keyboard alternatives, menu navigation, click paths, reduced motion, text scaling and fallback are implemented. Full key rebinding and screen-reader testing by a human remain unverified.
- CI jobs/artifacts are bounded. Public demo availability remains subject to GitHub Pages quotas and account permissions; see BUILD_STATE.md for actual release evidence.
