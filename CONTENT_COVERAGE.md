# Authored campaign coverage

Content edition: `twaw-2026.10.03-v1`. **24 permanent competencies, 12 fictional accounts, 40 NPCs, 52 missions, 104 authored evidence/verification documents and seven Bootcamp sessions.** Each normal case has seven action stages; `M-T01` has eight. The authoring schema and reference validator are in `src/content`, with content verification in `tests/content.test.ts`.

Every permanent competency supplies explanation, worked example, at least three glossary terms, misconception, guided practice, independent practice, rubric, source IDs and reusable work product. The campaign alternates tax and relationship lessons in the initial order. Study is non-punitive. Authored decisions include more than one defensible action; answer positions rotate. Communication steps expose a five-part draft/self-review rubric and a model answer. Free text is not automatically judged as expert work.

Shared capstones:

- `C01`: first independent day across HarborWorks, Cedarline and Forgebridge; evidence, arithmetic, backup and finite capacity.
- `C02`: Northlight historical notice, Cedarline data failure and a promised executive meeting; scarce specialist review and accountable recovery.
- `C03`: Forgebridge renewal, departing champion, new CFO, disputed value and new-plant scope.
- `C04`: Northlight acquisition with separate entities, incomplete history, bundled supply and EU/UK/Canadian launch gates.

Bootcamp covers all 24 core families in sessions 1–6. Session 7 introduces independent portfolio work and advanced prerequisites leading to `C02`. Free practice, replay, review queue and mentor functions use the same content and engine. Core/advanced/capstone stages unlock game titles only; they confer no professional credential.

Accounts preserve `acct-harborworks`, `acct-cedarline`, `acct-forgebridge`, `acct-atlasfield`, `acct-meridian` and `acct-northlight`. Additional accounts are Lumenleaf Analytics, Pinecrest Outdoor, Riverton Parts, Solstice Media, Bayshore Robotics and Aster Events. Each has three stakeholders with business goals, records they know, authority, availability and preferred communication. Four internal NPCs cover mentor, tax, operations and commercial roles. These authored attributes are not psychological predictions.

Eight calculation steps use explicit integer minor units or integer percentages: same-period credit, location allocation, direct-channel payable, duplicate control bridge, net VAT/GST under stated eligibility, inclusive invoice tax, revised late-credit draft and cohort NRR. Assumed rates and definitions are visibly synthetic. Legal sources remain professional-review pending, so cases grade investigation and bounded actions rather than real liability conclusions.

Validation checks schema shape, required permanent IDs, three stakeholders per account, missing source/document/contact/account references, orphan documents and competencies, impossible availability, missing calculation assumptions, communication model/rubric fields, future evidence references, duplicate IDs/fingerprints, passing branches and prerequisite cycles. Tests deliberately introduce each class of defect. The integration owner's release report records actual test execution.

The case families reuse an execution structure (prepare, discover, investigate, coordinate, communicate, follow up, debrief) while their source records, decision problems, calculations, specialist questions and work products differ. Later-stage documents are authored simulated verification outcomes, not external provider acceptance. Multi-day cases and business deadlines are compressed training narratives; no real calendar, CRM, tax system, bank or learner cloud is connected. Professional content review and actual other-edition interoperability remain pending.
